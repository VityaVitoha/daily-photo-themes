Privacy Policy

Daily Photo Themes

This Privacy Policy explains how Daily Photo Themes handles information when you use the extension and related website.

Information We Collect

Daily Photo Themes does not collect or sell personal information. The topic entered by the user is stored locally in the user's browser using local storage.

Photos

When a user enters a topic, the extension searches for an image using the Openverse API. Images are loaded from external sources and are not uploaded to our servers.

Pinterest

If Pinterest integration is enabled, the application may use the Pinterest API and OAuth authorization to provide Pinterest-related functionality requested by the user.

Pinterest authentication information is used only for the requested functionality and is not sold or shared for advertising purposes.

Third-Party Services

Daily Photo Themes may communicate with third-party services such as Openverse and Pinterest in order to provide its functionality.

Data Storage

User preferences and selected topics may be stored locally in the browser. Users can remove this information by clearing the extension's local storage or browser data.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the project's GitHub repository.
