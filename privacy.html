Privacy Policy

Daily Photo Themes

This Privacy Policy explains how Daily Photo Themes handles information when you use the application.

Information We Collect

Daily Photo Themes does not collect or store personal information on our servers.

The topic entered by the user and the selected image information may be stored locally in the user's browser using local storage.

Use of Pinterest

If Pinterest integration is enabled, the application may connect to Pinterest through the Pinterest API. Any access to Pinterest data is performed according to Pinterest's API policies and the permissions granted by the user.

Third-Party Services

The application may use third-party services to search for and display images. These services may receive the search topic submitted by the user.

Data Security

We do not intentionally collect personal information through Daily Photo Themes. Users should not enter sensitive or confidential information into the application.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application changes. Any updated version will be published on this page.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the project's GitHub repository.
